TITLE: Project Roadmap Q4

The LeakGuard Gateway project will move into its second development phase
starting Q4. Planned milestones include integrating the role-based RAG
pipeline with the existing document store, expanding detector coverage to
cover additional regional ID formats, and running a pilot with the
finance team before a company-wide rollout.

Engineering is targeting a feature-complete internal beta by the end of
November, followed by a two-week hardening period focused on latency and
false-positive rates in the detectors. Marketing and sales are not yet
briefed on the external launch timeline; that discussion is scheduled for
the next internal leadership sync and should not be shared outside the
engineering and product teams until then.

Budget for the Q4 phase has been allocated internally and covers two
additional contractor-engineers for the RAG and detector workstreams.

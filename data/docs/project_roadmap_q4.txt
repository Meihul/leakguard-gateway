TITLE: Project Roadmap Q4

This is the internal roadmap for the last quarter of the year. It is for employees only and should not be shared outside the company.

Project 1: Customer Portal Redesign
Owner: Rohan Mehta (Product). Deadline: 30 October. The new portal will have a simpler dashboard and faster login. The design is complete and development is 60 percent done. Main risk: delay in payment gateway testing.

Project 2: Mobile App Version 3.0
Owner: Priya Nair (Engineering). Deadline: 20 November. Features include offline mode, push notifications and dark theme. Beta testing with 200 internal users starts on 5 November. Main risk: limited testers on Android.

Project 3: Data Warehouse Migration
Owner: Arjun Kapoor (Data Team). Deadline: 10 December. Move all reports from the old server to the new cloud warehouse. The team has migrated 4 of 9 report groups. Main risk: old reports with missing documentation.

Project 4: Internal Security Audit
Owner: Sneha Iyer (IT Security). Deadline: 15 December. Review access rights, laptop encryption and vendor security. The audit report will go to the leadership team in the first week of January.

Weekly progress reviews are held every Thursday at 4:00 PM in the main meeting room.

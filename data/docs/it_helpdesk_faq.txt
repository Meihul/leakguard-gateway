TITLE: IT Helpdesk FAQ

This FAQ answers the most common IT questions. For anything not covered here, raise a ticket on the IT portal.

Q1. How do I reset my password?
Go to the company login page and click "Forgot Password". Enter your work email and follow the steps sent to your registered email. Choose a new password with at least 12 characters, including a number and a symbol. Never share your password with anyone, including the IT team. IT staff will never ask for it.

Q2. How do I connect to the VPN?
Install the company VPN client from the software portal. Open it, enter your work email, and approve the sign-in on your authenticator app. You need the VPN to reach internal tools when working from home. If it keeps disconnecting, restart the client and check your internet connection.

Q3. My laptop is slow or not starting. What should I do?
Restart it first and close any unused programs. If it still does not work, save your files to the shared drive if you can, then raise a ticket with the laptop's asset tag number, which is printed on the bottom of the device. For a broken screen or battery, visit the IT desk on the second floor.

Q4. How do I report a suspicious email?
Do not click any links. Use the "Report Phishing" button in your email app, or forward it to the IT security mailbox.

Helpdesk hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM on working days.

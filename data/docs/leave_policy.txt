TITLE: Leave Policy

All full-time employees of the company are entitled to paid leave every calendar year. This policy explains the types of leave, the number of days, and how to apply.

Types of leave and number of days:
1. Casual Leave: 12 days per year. Use it for personal work or short family needs. It cannot be carried forward to the next year.
2. Sick Leave: 10 days per year. If you are absent for more than 2 days in a row, a medical certificate is required.
3. Earned Leave: 18 days per year. It builds up month by month, and up to 30 unused days can be carried forward.
4. Maternity Leave: 26 weeks of paid leave. Paternity Leave: 10 working days.
5. Public Holidays: the company follows a list of 12 public holidays, published every January.

How to apply for leave:
1. Log in to the HR portal and click on "Leave".
2. Select the type of leave and choose your start and end dates.
3. Write a short reason and click Submit.
4. Your reporting manager will approve or reject the request within 2 working days.
5. For casual leave, please apply at least 1 day in advance. For earned leave, apply at least 7 days in advance.

In an emergency, inform your manager by phone or message first, and then apply on the portal within 2 days of returning to work. Leave without approval may be treated as unpaid leave. For any questions about this policy, please contact the HR team.

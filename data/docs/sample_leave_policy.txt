TITLE: Employee Leave Policy

All full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per
calendar year, in addition to public holidays observed by the company.
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least five
working days in advance, except in cases of medical emergency.

Unused annual leave may be carried forward into the next calendar year up
to a maximum of 5 days; any balance beyond that is forfeited on
December 31st. Employees are encouraged to plan their leave in
consultation with their reporting manager to ensure adequate team
coverage.

Sick leave is capped at 12 days per year and does not carry forward.
A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive
days. Maternity leave follows the statutory entitlement of 26 weeks, and
paternity leave is granted for 2 weeks. All leave policies apply equally
to interns, managers, and HR staff, and are reviewed annually by the HR
department.

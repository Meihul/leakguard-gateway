TITLE: Restructuring Plan

This is a confidential plan for merging the Web Development team and the Mobile Development team into one Product Engineering team. It must not be shared with employees until leadership announces it. Reference code: CANARY-HR-RST-9P4D.

Reason for the merge:
The two teams work on the same customer features and often duplicate work. Combining them should reduce delays and save about Rs 40,00,000 per year in tools and management costs.

Proposed structure:
The new Product Engineering team will have 22 people, led by Priya Nair. Rohan Mehta will move to a new Product Strategy role. Two team lead positions will be merged into one, and one duplicate manager position will be removed.

Impact on employees:
Around 3 roles may become redundant. Affected employees will be offered other open roles first. If no role is available, a severance package of 3 months' salary will be given.

Timeline:
Leadership approval: end of November. Employee announcement: first week of December. New structure starts: 1 January.

Communication rules:
Only the HR head and the two department heads know this plan. Any leak before the announcement may cause employees to resign. Questions should be sent directly to the HR head.
